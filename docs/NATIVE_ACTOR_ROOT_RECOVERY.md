Status: architectural prerequisites, not the complete room executor or V1 approval.

ports. A retained root selects its genuine client set; copied source objects,
replacement getters, unrelated cores and saved family labels cannot supply actor
ownership. NativeActorRetirement checks actual live actor custody and settled I/O
before irreversible retirement. The caller must keep the complete native/packet
barrier, recheck after the last authority callback, stop every Core and take the
actual cohort synchronously before old parent cleanup releases any actor.

origin-wide document/comment author locks, including while a replacement fails.

be split into partial ownership commits. New tests include65 simultaneous actual
