# Local operational participant cohort

Legacy local, local snapshot and flat-local parents can transfer their actual
native writers and complete upload stores in one in-process handoff. They use
the existing room handoff kernel. Each file family keeps its original meaning;
this transfer does not activate a server or reconcile a room's other copies.

The later [delivery and local cohort](DELIVERY_OPERATION_COHORT.md) adds genuine
complete-delivery and restored-operation parents through the same kernel. Its
53-stage qualification also rechecks this three-family cohort and repairs final
cancellation observation shared by its borrowed read scopes.

## Reservation and current state

The private router admits a bounded, indexed collection with distinct IDs and
genuine, correctly labelled parents. It refuses empty or sparse collections,
duplicate owners, saved readiness objects and wrong-family parents. Caller
array traversal overrides cannot omit a supplied member.

Every parent becomes busy synchronously before host policy verification starts.
Preparation verifies retained recovery bytes, journal state, native identity and
the complete upload store while holding the actual native and upload owners.
Upload leases already held by these families are not acquired a second time.

Result wrappers are constructed while closed. Current authority checks and
temporary-reader/coordinator cleanup run before the final callback-free pass.
That pass checks every native/storage witness after the last member's callback,
including independent document and comment generation changes. Only then does
the kernel consume every reservation synchronously. A failed policy, wrapper,
cleanup or current-state check consumes no member; the real owners remain
available for retry rather than being recreated from a marker.

## Provider and opening lifetime

Legacy local parts retain their `original` provider. Snapshot and flat-local
parts retain their `input` provider. The selected document policy remains in the
source type. Literal files, native histories, versions, original-only pixels and
upload receipts are not relabelled or rebuilt from the visible document tree.

Retired parents do not close returned sources or their journal-owned providers.
A provider follows its actual source lifetime; an original file is provenance,
not a backup of later edits. Independently retained Blob values stay caller-owned.

The legacy parent remains unavailable during construction and rechecks current
native/policy state after construction before publication. Failed openings keep
their genuine durable phases for explicit recovery. They do not return a partly
validated owner or infer operational readiness from a retained row alone.

## Bounds and functional coverage

The router admits at most 1,024 members. Child storage, journal, native-history
and asset limits remain unchanged. No public package-root export, serialized
protocol, default storage choice or native runtime is changed.

The fixed workload runs sixty three-writer cohorts: five normal native compiler
profiles, four document/comment history pairs and three cyclic family orders.
Selection scopes and capability masks rotate on a fixed schedule, not their full
Cartesian product. Each source, installed-package and actual production-bundle
run preserves 360 handoffs and exact native/upload/file/pixel comparisons, with
180 cold continuations, later document/comment edits, retained versions and
Undo/Redo exercises in both history lanes.

The full 480-case legacy parent, journal and portable-export source/installed
domains remain separate checks. The two-family snapshot/flat workload and
incoming-source/catalog integration also remain required. Controlled storage
does not establish physical durability.

Complete seven-family physical catalog writer adoption, retained-copy and live
provider/client reconciliation, authenticated activation and sustainable native
history remain open. This private functional contract does not close the V1
release gates or establish full feature parity.
