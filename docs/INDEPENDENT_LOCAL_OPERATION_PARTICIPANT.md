# Local operational participant ownership

A complete local operational participant joins the actual native save journal,
whole upload store and original image provider. It keeps both independent native
histories, local versions and outstanding offline work without inventing a relay
binding, receipt or server activation. The private contract uses a new parent
format; existing local recovery and operation formats keep their meanings.

## Names and current policy

The host selects six distinct namespaces: the completed recovery attempt, its
retired read-only source, uploads, the new participant parent, its native journal
and its editable native source. The recovery selection supplies fresh read-only
replay factories, limits, current synchronous authority and optional cancellation.
A separate current source factory selects editing permissions under the same
native identity. Files and saved phases select neither executable factories nor
permissions.

The parent reserves all six names before authority callbacks. Its genuine runtime
opening may delegate only the exact journal/source pair to one journal open. A
structural object or serialized flag cannot delegate custody. Failed child cleanup
stays with the real parent rollback ledger until it settles; ordinary overlapping
opens still refuse.

## Transfer from actual recovery

Begin accepts the genuine completed local recovery owner. While its read-only
source, uploads, original archive and three namespace leases remain held, the
parent retains an absent-only intent and admits its separate native journal.
The old source remains live through the recovery owner's final native and upload
checks. Only successful actual transfer permits closing that source and settling
its author leases before opening the new source.

The new parent record advances from preparing to prepared to operational. The
first two phases permit only the exact original native pair, versions and upload
snapshot. An original descendant is not sufficient proof for first ownership.
Operational publication requires actual source/journal and upload verification;
the record alone never establishes readiness.

Resume from a still-held recovery owner is separate from cold resume. It admits
the same genuine old owner and reconciles an interrupted parent/journal prepare.
Cold resume re-admits the actual completed recovery, retired native source,
original archive, native journal and whole uploads under fresh host policy.
Missing acknowledged components refuse without deletion or reconstruction.

## Live source and provider lifetime

The new source owns the native journal and its separately admitted original
archive/provider. Parent handoff must not dispose those resources or bind future
saves to the parent's closed flag or the obsolete recovered owner's grant.
The handed-off original provider is borrowed from the source and closes with it.
The parent separately owns uploads, upload exclusion, retained recovery handles
and namespace leases until final handoff.

After operational publication, new upload rows and legitimate retries may
advance. Original bytes, MIME, dimensions and acknowledged uploaded references
must remain, and attempts/epochs cannot move backward. Original-only provider
bytes remain distinct from the upload queue.

## Final handoff and cleanup

Handoff holds actual upload exclusion and a genuine native read witness across
every awaited namespace release. After the last authority callback it compares
the exact native checkpoints, version strings and source stamp, then releases
upload custody and returns without another await or host callback. A failed
barrier retains the actual owners for retry. Retiring archive wrappers precedes
that barrier and does not remove the immutable baseline needed for retry.

The private [local operational cohort](LOCAL_OPERATION_COHORT.md) reserves this
actual parent together with snapshot and flat-local parents through the same
room kernel. It keeps all genuine upload leases and native read scopes until
every member passes the final callback-free comparison. Legacy `original`
providers keep their meaning. The parent also remains unavailable during
construction and rechecks its current grant before publication.

Source close settles the same real native and journal leases. Failed asynchronous
release is retryable cleanup, not permanent cached failure or native resurrection.
Explicit discard may abandon unsaved memory but never deletes acknowledged
storage. Failed unreturned opens remain in their actual opening cleanup ledger.

## Verification and remaining room work

Functional laws must cover all four independent document/comment history pairs,
future Undo/Redo after offline peer work, local versions, whole uploads and
original-only image bytes through live/cold handoff. Interrupt parent and native
publication, retry genuine held recovery and failed cleanup, and reject missing,
forked, rollback or unrelated storage before autosave.

This participant is not a complete portable local operation archive. That export
must explicitly retain original/current/pending/live native data, whole uploads
and provider provenance. Complete seven-family native room adoption, retained-copy/client
reconciliation, authenticated activation and sustainable native history remain
separate requirements. Existing history limits are unchanged.
