# Native room union execution components

Breditor can durably retain the complete mixed room proof and both explicit whole-upload decisions, retire a genuinely held cohort while retaining its author locks, and restore ordinary target participants through those same locks. The ordered native and asset handoff reaches a usable selected owner without losing the compatible foreign edits or local Undo and Redo.

These are SDK-private execution components. They do not yet form a complete union-aware warm/cold run coordinator with a durable progress journal. Local ownership is not authenticated server activation. The existing exact-preparation, execution and run protocols keep their original meanings and defaults; a mixed proof is never disguised as one of them.

## Three complete upload inputs

Every member retains selected-before, selected-retained and the queue attached to its actual prepared native backup. The first decision edge reconciles the two selected queues. The second reconciles the native queue with that selected result. Conflicting states require explicit choices on each edge, and every losing complete input remains available in the saved bases.

The two previews expose those edges separately. The second requires the complete first choice; no callback silently chooses which failed, cancelled, pending or uploaded record survives. Capture reproduces both full bases for every member under the tighter applicable original and current limits. Cold admission rechecks their literal input bytes, source databases, policies, decisions and output catalog. Swapping two otherwise valid bases cannot exchange those obligations.

An archived decision may tighten, but not widen, either source policy. A fresh reader may tighten admission further without rewriting the archived decision. Stored receipts remain data rather than newly authenticated provider acknowledgements.

## Explicit execution identity

The new execution archive contains the complete mixed preparation and two full bases per participant. Its selection is total bytes plus manifestSha256, not a whole-file hash. The nested preparation keeps its own manifest identity and is semantically re-admitted through its reader. Normal asset components retain complete SHA-256 pins, exact contiguous extents and the unchanged native digest bounds.

This aggregate is bounded at 32 GiB, its canonical header at 32 MiB and the cohort at 1024 participants. Native component hashing still stops at 1 GiB and uses 1 MiB chunks. These bounds do not constitute memory, quota, maximum-size or latency qualification.

## Preparation before retirement

Inspection holds the actual complete selected cohort and reads the existing immutable mixed preparation. It saves no target. Preparation freshly rebuilds the selected execution file, checks every future namespace is absent, verifies the actual source cut before and after saving, and creates only one absent-only immutable execution journal.

Existing-only reopen never creates a missing placeholder. Every reopen repeats native, store and full queue-edge admission under fresh original-role windows and current policy. Storage and lock identities are pinned before host callbacks. An aborted upgrade leaves no empty intent; a committed file survives a lost completion. Count-first reads reject unexpected rows before loading the large blob.

## Genuine writer retirement

retireNativeRoomUnionKeepingActors reads the existing mixed journal, re-admits its full contents and rebuilds every declared recipe against the actual held live cohort. Missing roles, a changed current source, an occupied known target or a missing journal refuses before approval. Approval must be a fresh affirmative executable decision, not stored data.

The final retirement capture is deliberately one-shot. The genuine owner retains its complete native and packet witness through final permission callbacks and cleanup; it refuses retirement without that witness. The wrapper must not recapture or replace it with a detached comparison. Actual native retirement is synchronous before the shared exclusion ends, and durable cleanup completes outside the visitor promise.

All author resources stay with the same canonical owner after a cleanup failure. Taking them before cleanup succeeds refuses. The reserved alternative requires the actual original-owner reservation, preventing competing retirement or public closure during an asynchronous handoff. Neither report exposes author custody, a replacement editor or a serving method. Namespace absence is an observation, not a lasting target reservation; concrete recovery must still admit its own absent or exact destinations.

## Target and selected owner handoff

A fresh scoped reader converts the semantically admitted mixed preparation into an ordinary genuine catalog. Concrete target recovery consumes that catalog with independently selected host policies and the actual stopped actor cohort. No old exact archive is fabricated, and ordinary writer reacquisition is unnecessary. Closing and reopening staged targets leaves the author locks with their genuine retained owner until whole-cohort commit.

The prepared adoption bases then reach the existing native and selected-asset transfer barrier. That barrier owns an actual successor recipient before activation, verifies the complete source and selected queues, and transfers all author resources together. The selected owner uses its separate post-handoff policy rather than the ended read job. The functional composition checks foreign text/comments, local future Redo and a separately cancelled selected queue after the old read scope closes.

## Remaining architecture

A complete union-aware warm/cold run coordinator must persist the chosen layout, progress, interruption disposition and completion boundary around these components. The ordered functional composition is not that crash/restart protocol. Existing exact-run identities cannot stand in for the new execution choice, and saved completion cannot supply actor acquisition or serving authority.

Original-role network authentication, provider/client closure, independent comment and server-log generation lifecycle and authenticated whole-room activation remain required. Other operational families still need union mutation; unknown own-author suffixes and incomparable sealed branches retain their existing refusal semantics. Sustainable history, retained versions/assets and all V1 gates remain open.

Reproducible focused commands are scripts/check-native-room-union-handoff-sdk.mjs and scripts/check-native-room-union-handoff-installed.mjs. They reverify pinned real native fixture data and exercise actual Wasm with controlled IndexedDB, locks and raster presentation. They do not claim fresh Rust compilation or physical power-loss qualification.
